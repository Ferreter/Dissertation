### RQ1: Direction of the Final-Hour SPX Return

Nested expanding-window tuning identified Histogram Gradient Boosting, trained using the relaxed dataset and full feature set, as the leading directional specification. The model achieved a mean outer-fold balanced accuracy of 0.544, with a standard deviation of 0.070. Increasing the random-search budget from 8 to 30 candidate configurations did not improve its mean performance, although the same model, dataset variant and feature set remained selected. This provides limited evidence that intraday market features contain information about the direction of the final-hour SPX return. However, the small improvement above the neutral balanced-accuracy benchmark of 0.50 and the variation across temporal folds indicate that the relationship is weak and unstable over time.

### RQ2: Magnitude of the Final-Hour SPX Movement

The leading magnitude specification was an RBF Support Vector Regressor trained using the relaxed dataset and reduced feature set. It achieved a mean outer-fold MAE of 13.97 basis points, a mean out-of-sample (R^2) of 0.003 and a mean Spearman correlation of 0.373. The near-zero (R^2) indicates that the model explained very little of the variation in the absolute size of final-hour movements. Nevertheless, the positive Spearman correlation suggests some ability to rank sessions according to their relative expected movement magnitude. The results therefore provide limited evidence of ranking ability, rather than evidence that the model can accurately predict the absolute magnitude of the final-hour SPX movement. Comparison with a fold-matched median benchmark is required to determine whether the reduction in prediction error is meaningful.
